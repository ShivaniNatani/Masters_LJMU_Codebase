# Untrained (Random-Initialisation) Control Condition — Provenance Note

**Status:** Archived deliberately. Retained as a legitimate experimental control.
**Archived:** 26 July 2026

## What these files are

These results were generated by `scripts/evaluate_mimic_real.py` and
`scripts/evaluate_iu_real.py` **before** those scripts loaded a trained
checkpoint. At the time of the run, both scripts constructed the model as:

    model = LabelGuidedMedicalVLM(...).to(device)

with no `load_state_dict` call. The evaluated model therefore had:

- BioMedCLIP vision encoder — pretrained, frozen (as designed)
- FLAN-T5-Base decoder — pretrained (as designed)
- **Vision-language projection MLP — randomly initialised, untrained**
- **LoRA adapters — freshly initialised, untrained**

## Evidence

1. Neither script contained a checkpoint-loading call, while the three
   mock-data eval scripts (`evaluate_baseline`, `evaluate_rag`,
   `evaluate_label_guided`) each contained two.
2. `logs/execution.log`: MIMIC evaluation ran 21:25:07–21:42:48. MIMIC
   training Stage 1 Epoch 1 did not complete until 22:05:41. Checkpoints in
   `checkpoints/mimic_real/` are timestamped 22:40 — after the results.
3. Generated text is degenerate and repetitive, e.g.
   "X-ray image of a patient with a chest X-ray image of a patient with a
   chest X-ray image of a patient with a chest..."
4. Baseline BLEU-1 through BLEU-4 are exactly 0.0000 on MIMIC-CXR.

## Sample size caveat

`max_samples` was hardcoded to 200 in `run_condition_eval`. These files
therefore cover **200 test cases per condition**, NOT the full test splits
(MIMIC-CXR n=2,922; IU n=1,180). The superseded report
`docs/archive/FINAL_CROSS_DATASET_DISSERTATION_REPORT_UNTRAINED_SUPERSEDED.md`
incorrectly states the full test-split sizes.

## Why it is scientifically useful

This is a random-initialisation control. Because the projection and LoRA
weights carry no learned image-text mapping, any measured performance in the
RAG and Combined conditions cannot come from multimodal grounding. It must
come from the decoder copying retrieved report text out of the prompt.

The condition therefore provides a **copy-baseline / prompt-leakage floor**:
it quantifies how much of the apparent benefit of retrieval augmentation is
attributable to surface-level copying rather than learned visual grounding.
Trained-model gains should be interpreted relative to this floor, not
relative to zero.

## Correct interpretation in the dissertation

- Do NOT cite these numbers as the performance of the proposed system.
- DO cite them as the untrained/copy-only control condition.
- The trained results (produced after the checkpoint-loading fix) are the
  primary experimental results.

## Fix applied

Both eval scripts now: (a) load `checkpoints/{mimic_real,iu_real}/baseline_best_loss.pt`
and abort if absent, (b) evaluate the full test split by default with an
`EVAL_MAX_SAMPLES` override, and (c) persist per-sample predictions to
`results/{mimic,iu}_real_sample_predictions.csv`.
